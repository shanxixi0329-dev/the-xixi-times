AI · 开源

Mistral 发布 Large 4：公司称开源权重版在编码基准居前

约一万亿参数原生多模态模型；Ollama 邮件同步宣传可托管调用。以下为公司说法。

据 mistral.ai 官方稿 · Ollama 产品信 · 公司基准未独立核实

公司口径，未独立核实

Mistral announced Large 4 (“Le Chonk”), a roughly one-trillion-parameter native multimodal model, and released improved open weights. The company says the open-weight edition leads open-weight coding benchmarks and strengthens defensive cybersecurity skills, with three reasoning modes. Ollama’s product mail promoted hosting and serving the model; full local quantized runs remain hardware-heavy.

编辑摘要 把它当「又一个可自托管的大模型入口」来读，而不是立刻换生产底座。公司编码与网络安全口径未独立核实；许可证与商用条款要自己核对。对本地实验者，先看量化体积与显存，再谈「满血」。

中国 · 政策

中国拟控约 23 项 AI 技术出口，征求意见至 11 月 15 日

覆盖先进模型、智能体、芯片设计与大型算力集群等；API/网页/App/SaaS 访问大体豁免。

据 AP / Reuters 系报道 · 商务部征求意见稿

China’s draft export controls would cover about 23 AI-related technologies, including advanced models, agents, chip-design tools and large compute clusters. Overseas transfers of qualifying model weights, source code, data and expertise could require approval or registration, while access through APIs, websites, apps and SaaS platforms is generally allowed. Public comment closes November 15, 2026; a temporary registration requirement for certain transfers took effect October 16.

编辑摘要 这条是昨纸的续报，节点没变：目录仍在征求意见，登记已经开始。「卖访问、留权重」的分界仍是读稿关键。定稿与执法口径以 11 月 15 日后文本为准。

中国 · 区域合作

中越探讨 AI 联合研究：LLM、开源与产学对接

工信部官员在越南国际数字周相关场合提出合作意向；越方表述长期目标含跻身区域与全球前列。

据 TechNode Global 2026-10-30

At Vietnam’s International Digital Week events, a Chinese MIIT official floated joint research on large language models, open source, and industry–academia links. Vietnamese officials restated ambitions to become a leading Southeast Asian AI hub by 2030 and among the global top ten by 2045.

编辑摘要 目前是意向与场合表态，不是已落地的联合实验室清单。对读者有用的是：开源与产学被点名为合作抓手，可与国内出口管制稿对照——一边谈对外合作，一边收紧技术出境边界。

国际 · 网络安全

分析指朝鲜相关团伙实施史上最大规模加密货币盗窃

攻击针对全球大型交易所钱包；多家链上情报公司给出高置信归因。

据 AP / TRM Labs / Chainalysis / BBC

Blockchain intelligence firms TRM Labs and Chainalysis attributed with high confidence a record theft from Binance wallets—on the order of roughly six billion U.S. dollars moved across multiple chains—to a North Korea–linked unit often tracked as TraderTraitor / Lazarus-related. Binance described compromised engineer devices among the breach points, said a large share of funds was frozen, and stated it would cover user losses.

编辑摘要 当作国家级网络盗窃与交易所安全事件读，不当作投资建议。归因来自链上情报公司与通稿，不是法庭终裁；关注点是入侵路径与冻结/赔付口径是否兑现。
