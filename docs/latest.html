科技 · 中国

苹果拟为 Apple Intelligence 接入阿里通义千问

开发者 Beta 计划 11 月 4 日起测；面向中国用户的系统版本节点报道指向 12 月；覆盖 Siri、写作工具与视觉智能，可选开启，并称不用于训练。

据新华社等公开报道 · 本报摘要

Apple plans to integrate Alibaba’s Qwen model into Apple Intelligence features for users in China, according to state-media and follow-on coverage. A developer beta is described as starting around November 4, with a broader China-facing system release discussed for December. Public readouts mention Siri, writing tools and visual intelligence, frame the feature as optional, and say prompts would not be used for training.

编辑摘要 把它当「中国区本地大模型接入路线」而不是全球统一底座换芯。Beta 日历不是上线承诺；写作流先别为新闻改版。许可证、开关位置与隐私说明以苹果正式文档为准。

AI · 研究

DeepSeek 亮出 Super Agent：路由加多 worker 的研究架构

报道描述任务路由与并行 worker；公司侧口径偏研究原型，非正式产品发版。

据环球时报 / 南华早报等 11/01 报道 · 研究阶段表述

Coverage of DeepSeek’s Super Agent describes a router-plus-multi-worker design meant to split and parallelize complex tasks. Reports in early November 2026 frame it as a research architecture rather than a finished consumer product launch. Details on availability, pricing and safety layers remain thin in secondary write-ups.

编辑摘要 读成「多智能体怎么分工」的研究笔记，不要读成明天就能替换现有助手的发版公告。若你跟进，优先核对官方技术说明是否区分 demo 与可调用 API。

国际 · 外交

G20 外长会未发表联合公报

公开报道指向乌克兰局势与美伊相关议题上的分歧；当作短讯备忘。

据新华社 Daily World Briefing 等 11/01 · 本报摘要

G20 foreign ministers concluded meetings without a joint communique, according to early November briefings. Public coverage highlighted disagreements tied to the war in Ukraine and related U.S.–Iran diplomacy tracks. This paper logs the outcome as a short diplomatic note, not a full summit reconstruction.

编辑摘要 无公报本身就是信号：共识文本谈不拢时，各方会改走各自声明。纸面不臆造谈判细节；跟进以各方外交部口径为准。
