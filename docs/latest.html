天数智芯演示 Tiangai 300：公司称多模态负载可对标仍可对华出口的 H200

上海天数智芯近日演示；以下为公司说法，本报未独立核实。

据 SCMP / 芯智讯等对演示的转述 · 公司演示数据未独立核实

公司演示，未独立核实

Iluvatar CoreX (天数智芯), the Shanghai-based chip designer, told mainland media that its new Tiangai 300 GPU matches or surpasses Nvidia’s H200 on real multimodal workloads such as text-to-image and text-to-video with models including Z.ai’s GLM-5.3. The H200 remains among the most advanced Nvidia AI GPUs still cleared for export to China under current U.S. rules. The claims come from a company demonstration; independent benchmarks were not available at press time.

编辑摘要 天数智芯接着说，演示面向内地媒体，卖点是在仍可进口的 H200 这一档上对标。对读者有用的读法是：把它当成国产加速卡的公开表态，而不是已经坐实的替代结论。缺第三方复测前，纸面只报公司口径，并与出口管制稿对照——一边是本地算力叙事，一边是模型与技术出境收紧。

中国 · 政策

商务部拟将约 23 项 AI 技术写入禁止/限制出口目录，征求意见至 11 月 15 日

第 48 号公告同步要求相关技术出口前登记；多数 API、SaaS 与已公开开源不在大模型技术出口管控之列。

据商务部 / 科技部公告 · The Diplomat 分析

Announcement No. 48 from China’s Ministry of Commerce (with MOST) proposes adding about 23 AI technologies to the Catalogue of Technologies Prohibited or Restricted from Export. Public comment runs through November 15; pre-export registration is already in effect for listed items. The draft reaches advanced-model weights and source, AI agents, AI chip design, and interconnect/scheduling for clusters above roughly 10,000 accelerators. MOFCOM’s explanation says offering even restricted large models through APIs, apps, SaaS, or lightweight on-device deployments generally falls outside large-model technology-export controls; technology already in the public domain via open source or papers is excluded, though a first open-source release of restricted tech still counts as an export eligible for automatic licensing.

编辑摘要 商务部稿把「卖访问、留权重」写得很清楚：目录还在征求意见，但登记已经开始。覆盖范围点名模型权重与源码、智能体、AI 芯片设计，以及超大规模集群的互联与调度；同时给 API/云服务和多数公开开源留了口子。对做工具链与模型调用的人，短期更宜盯合规与登记实操。定稿与执法口径仍要以 11 月 15 日后文本为准。

中国 · 大模型

上半年中国 LLM MaaS：阿里云约 46.6%，智谱与火山引擎紧随

GeekPark 隔夜综合 IDC 等口径；腾讯预告 Hy4，并推出跨应用 agent 层 Buddy One。

据 GeekPark 隔夜综合 / 雷峰网转引 IDC · 份额为机构口径

Overnight China LLM notes (GeekPark, Oct 30): Alibaba Cloud took about 46.6% of China’s LLM MaaS market in H1 2026 (IDC via Leiphone); Zhipu 10.6%, Volcano Engine 10.2%. Tencent previewed Hy4 (vision) and shipped Buddy One, a cross-app agent layer. Doubao opened third-party models to enterprise stacks (Kimi 3.1 Pro, GLM 5.3, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash). Separately, Artificial Analysis v5 ranked open-weight DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and GLM 5.3 around 14th/15th — a signal, not a scoop.

编辑摘要 这条适合当市场位置图，不适合当单一产品评测。阿里云仍是 MaaS 大头；智谱与火山引擎份额接近。腾讯的视觉模型预告与跨应用 agent、以及豆包对企业开放第三方模型，说明谁在卖调用、谁在做编排同样值得盯。开源榜名次只作旁证；公司自报基准一律标明未核实。
